Community Guidelines

NOURIVA is an evidence-informed space. To keep discussions constructive and safe:

  • No medical diagnosis or prescription claims: Frame suggestions as general wellness learning.
  • Cite sources where possible: Link peer-reviewed studies or respected educational textbooks.
  • Respect diverse dietary contexts: Celebrate personal routines without dogma.
  • Zero commercial spam or affiliate links.
